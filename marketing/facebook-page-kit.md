## Page bio

**Short bio (Facebook "Intro", 101 characters):**

> Libreng CS Exam reviewer na may study bestie na walang filter: si Kiko 🌱

**Long "About" section:**

> **CS Coach** ang libreng review app para sa **Civil Service Exam (Professional)**. 📚
>
> Pero hindi ito ordinaryong reviewer. May kasama kang si **Kiko**, ang study bestie mong magaling, mabilis sumagot, at walang filter. 🌱
>
> 🤨 **Hindi ka niya paplastikin.** Kapag tamad ka, sasabihan ka niya ("May oras ka sa TikTok pero walang oras sa 10 questions?"). Kapag pasado ka, magchi-cheer siya. Kapag malungkot ka, hindi siya mang-aasar, mag-aalaga siya.
>
> ✅ **Hindi mo na kailangang lumabas ng app.** May tanong, may hindi maintindihan, may term na hindi mo alam? Itanong mo na kay Kiko, nasa loob mismo ng app. Walang tab-hopping, walang Google, walang Facebook scroll na hahantong sa 2 oras na pagkaligaw.
>
> **Ano pa ang kasama:**
> ⚡ Practice mode na may agarang explanation, o Exam mode na parang totoong exam
> 🎯 Full Mock Exam at subject rounds (Verbal, Numerical, Analytical, General Info)
> 📊 Analytics at weakest topics, para alam mo kung saan ka bumabagsak
> 🗓️ Exam countdown, daily goal at streak
> 💡 "Help me" hint sa bawat tanong, gagabayan ka niya nang hindi ibibigay ang sagot
> 🇵🇭 English, Tagalog o Taglish ang interface
> 📱 Gumagana sa phone, pwedeng i-install, at hindi nawawala ang progress mo
>
> Pasado ang goal: **80%**. Si Kiko na ang bahala sa pang-aasar. 😎
>
> *Hindi kami affiliated sa Civil Service Commission. Ang mga tanong ay practice items na hango sa official exam coverage.*
>
> 👉 [ilagay ang link ng app dito]

**What makes it different (for replies and "what's this?" comments):**

- Kiko is a buddy with a personality, not a boring quiz screen. He cheers, teases and comforts depending on how you are doing.
- Answers and explanations stay inside the app, so you don't leave to search.
- It reads your own scores and tells you the weak subject by name.
- It is free, in Taglish, and built for Filipino exam takers.

> **Heads-up before you post:** Kiko's built-in replies (the sarcasm, hints, score analysis, study plans) work out of the box. The "ask him anything and he explains it" part (like the Habeas corpus screenshot) needs the user to connect a **free Google Gemini key** once in Settings. That is why the bio says "itanong mo kay Kiko" and not "sasagutin ka ng AI agad." If you want to promise the AI part with no setup, the AI proxy in `server/` needs to be deployed first.

## Tips for traction

- **Post the Kiko screenshots first.** Roasts get shared. The analytics tab does not.
- **Ask a question** at the end of the post, e.g. "Anong sinabi ni Kiko sa'yo? I-screenshot at i-comment!"
- **Pin** caption 20 (the "tag your friend" post) to the top of the page.
- **Video:** screen-record a round where you answer wrong on purpose and let Kiko react. A 10-second clip like that will probably do better than any still image.
- Replies sound best in Taglish, the same voice as Kiko.
